Privacy Policy

Common Sense: 1776 · Effective October 4, 2026 · Support

The short version

Common Sense: 1776 collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved game.
  • Your settings, such as music on or off, the Cinematics setting and the map layer.
  • Which guide cards and scenes you have already seen.

These are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. On the New Game screen, “Reset all choices” clears the settings and the scenes seen.

Links

The About screen has links to this page, the support page and the license of one set of sounds. They open in your web browser; the game itself sends nothing.

Children

The game is written for students. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
