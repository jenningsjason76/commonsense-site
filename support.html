Common Sense: 1776

Support · Privacy Policy

Common Sense: 1776 is a strategy game about the American Revolution, written to help students prepare for AP U.S. History (Period 3). Lead the Continental Congress, or direct Britain's war from London.

Contact

Email commonsense@longroomprojects.com. Please include the version shown in the app's About screen (on the title screen) and your device.

Requirements

  • iPhone and iPad: iOS / iPadOS 17 or later.
  • Mac: macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
  • One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Common questions

How do I play as Britain? On the title screen choose New Game, then under “Play as” pick Britain, choose the computer's level and tap Start.

Where is my game saved? On your device only. The game keeps one saved game and continues it when you return. Saves are not synced between devices.

Can I shorten the cinematics? Yes: open the game menu (the three-line button at the top left) and choose Cinematics: Full, Battles only or Off.

Where are the sources and credits? History & Sources (on the title screen and in the game) holds the history notes with their citations, “What Really Happened”, and the credits for every picture and sound under Art & Music. The About screen lists the licenses.

Something looks wrong. Email us with what you were doing, the year and phase shown at the top of the screen, and a screenshot if you can.
